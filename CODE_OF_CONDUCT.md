# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, experience level, nationality, gender, religion or background.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive
- Accept feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community
- Help newcomers

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing other people's private information
- Spam or off-topic promotion
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that
violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for
behaviour they consider inappropriate.

## Reporting

Report violations privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/JustRohams). All reports are handled confidentially.
